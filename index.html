Tools & systems

Tools built from
production bottlenecks.

Most of these tools started the same way: a repetitive manual task became slow, inconsistent or difficult to review at scale. Instead of adding more process around the problem, I built a tool to remove the bottleneck.

01 Blender · Review

Texture Reviewer

The blocker

Large material families were slow to validate manually. Wrong color spaces, inconsistent map ratios and subtle differences between versions were easy to miss.

What I built

A controlled review scene with original/remaster comparison, HDRI switching, family navigation, non-color validation and aspect-ratio checks.

02 Blender · Pipeline

Asset Organizer & Exporter

The blocker

Versions, collections and export states were being managed by hand. That made naming drift, duplicate objects and inconsistent deliveries too easy.

What I built

A standardized original/remaster collection structure with controlled instancing, predictable naming and repeatable export of production-ready props.

03 Blender · QA

Prop Reviewer

The blocker

A remastered asset can look convincing in isolation while still drifting from the source in silhouette, proportion or placement.

What I built

A fast review setup for original-versus-remaster comparison, focused on silhouette, scale, orientation and high-level shape before detail review.

04 Web · QA

Render Compare

The blocker

Small lighting, material and exposure changes are difficult to judge from memory, especially across many iterations or reviewers.

What I built

A lightweight A/B comparison tool for instant visual validation of render versions, making differences obvious without switching files or applications.

05 Three.js · GLTF

Interactive Product Viewer

The blocker

Static renders do not expose how a configurable product behaves: material states, moving parts, hidden surfaces and browser performance all matter.

What I built

A browser-based GLTF viewer with material variants, doors and drawers, camera controls, product states, wall visibility logic and web-oriented asset handling.

06 Python · Optimization

Batch Texture Processor

The blocker

Oversized source textures, mixed formats and inconsistent map resolutions created unnecessary memory use and repeated manual cleanup.

What I built

Batch conversion and relinking scripts with map-specific resolution rules, consistent formats and predictable texture budgets for production delivery.
